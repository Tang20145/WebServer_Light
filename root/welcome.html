Why "JCGaming"?

The name "JCGaming" comes from my own name. My initials are "HJC" (in Chinese). Dropping the "H"(my last name initial) leaves me with "JC", which is the core of my identity. "Gaming" represents my passion for always seeking fun, excitement, and passion in everything I do. To me, life is a game, and I'm always striving for that next level of joy and discovery.
